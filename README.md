## ✨ Features & Magic

1. **💖 Opening Screen**
   - Soft pink ambient glow with floating aurora orbs.
   - Glowing pulsing heart animation with orbiting micro-hearts.
   - Smooth navigation into our story with scroll hints.

2. **⏱️ Live Counter & Heartbeat Meter**
   - Real-time live counter calculating Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds since October 10, 2024.
   - Synchronized heartbeat pulse on every passing second.
   - Live Heartbeat ticker displaying over **63,000,000+ heartbeats together** with an animated pulsing EKG waveform.

3. **💌 Typewriter Love Letter**
   - Handwritten-style message that types itself out character-by-character with natural cadence pauses.
   - Animated vintage quill pen (`✒️`) that writes along with the typing cursor.
   - Interactive glowing wax seal (`♥`) that bursts with rose petals and heart sparkles when clicked.
   - "Read again ✨" replay button.

4. **🎞️ Cinematic Photo Slideshow**
   - Auto-rotating 10-photo slideshow with smooth fade and Ken Burns zoom effect.
   - Real-time animated glowing pink progress bar line (3 seconds per slide).
   - Drifting sunbeam / light leak flare effect across memories.
   - Touch swipe, navigation arrows, and interactive dot indicators.

5. **📸 Interactive Memory Album Gallery**
   - 10 memories in responsive rounded pink frames (2 columns on mobile, 3 on desktop).
   - Delicate swaying corner hearts (`💕`, `💖`, `🌹`, `✨`) and diagonal light reflection sweeps.
   - Full-screen Lightbox modal zoom on tap.
   - Direct inline caption editing (`✏️`) with instant preview.

6. **🎆 Grand Celebration & Kiss Buttons**
   - **"Celebrate 2 Years! 🎊"**: Launches full-screen fireworks on an HTML5 canvas with exploding hearts, golden stardust, and cascading flower petals.
   - **"Send a Kiss & Hearts 💋"**: Shoots a shower of kissing hearts across the screen.

7. **🎵 Built-in Romantic Music Box (Web Audio API)**
   - 100% self-contained pure JavaScript synthesizer using the browser's Web Audio API.
   - Plays a delicate music box chime melody with floating musical notes (`♪ ♫ ♬`).
   - Requires zero external audio files, zero MP3s, and zero internet connection!

8. **🪄 Touch Fairy Dust Trail & Love Bubbles**
   - Gliding your finger or cursor leaves a glowing trail of fairy dust particles.
   - Tapping anywhere pops romantic affirmation bubbles like *"Forever Yours, Ashen"*, *"Kaveesha & Ashen ✨"*, and *"My Whole World 💖"*.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Pure Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, & JavaScript**

- **Zero External Dependencies**: No CDNs, no jQuery, no frameworks, no external fonts.
